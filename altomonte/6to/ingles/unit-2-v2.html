📏 Superlative Rules

⚡ Short Adjectives

Para palabras cortitas, agregamos -est al final.

  • 🌲 Big ➔ The biggest
  • ⛰️ High ➔ The highest
  • *Si termina en 'y' cambia a 'i': Dry ➔ The driest

✨ Long Adjectives

Para palabras largas, ponemos The most antes de la palabra.

  • 🧊 Glacial ➔ The most glacial
  • 🏜️ Arid ➔ The most arid
  • 📸 Beautiful ➔ The most beautiful
