How to form superlatives

Short adjectives → add -est 1 or 2 syllables

Take the adjective, add the at the start and -est at the end.
big → the biggest · dry → the driest · long → the longest · high → the highest
⚠️ Double the final consonant when the adjective ends in consonant-vowel-consonant: big → biggest, wet → wettest

Long adjectives → use the most 3+ syllables

Put the most before the adjective. Never add -est.
glacial → the most glacial · arid → the most arid · extreme → the most extreme

Irregular adjectives — must be memorised!

good → the best · bad → the worst

North America & climate change

Remember the reading from Lesson 4! Climate change is affecting landscapes differently across North America:

In the North (Arctic)
Glaciers are melting → more snowstorms and glacial winds.
In the Centre (Colorado)
Landscape getting more arid → longer droughts, wildfires.
In the South (Puerto Rico)
Hurricanes more frequent and violent → floods and strong winds.
